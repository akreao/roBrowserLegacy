## Weapon look lost after class change

Fixed in ca53ff9. When the base type's sprite is drawn in place of the look's, the view now stores the requested look in `_weapon` rather than the base type. A later `UpdateBody` puts the look back on, so a class that has the look's sprite draws it. Attack action, sound and trail already resolve the base type from the look with `getWeaponType(id, true)`, so they are unchanged. A test in `tests/renderer/EntityWeaponExpansion.test.js` covers a Druid with look 31 changing to Knight.
