## Thread: Map prefetch bypasses download limit

Fixed in 1a6184e. Every file of a map load now goes through one queue shared by all the steps, with at most six loading at once, the same limit each step kept on its own before. A file a step is waiting for goes ahead of one asked for early, so the water images the ground step needs are not stuck behind the models. Each filename still has one request until a step takes it. A new test covers the limit and the order.

## Thread: Failed maps leak prefetched texture URLs

Fixed in 1a6184e. When the load fails (missing altitude or ground) or finishes, the queue is closed. It asks for nothing more, and it revokes the blob URL of every early texture no step took, both those already loaded and those that arrive afterwards. Two new tests cover the ground arriving before and after the altitude fails.

## Thread: Water images miss the prefetch window

Leaving this as it is. Whether a map draws water is known only once the ground is compiled (`waterVertCount`), so asking for the 32 images earlier would cost 32 requests on every map without water. With the new queue they now go ahead of the models still waiting, so the ground step does not wait behind them.
